# Deep-read quality checks

This is the release check for a **single-paper DEEP READ**. It applies to the source paper, Markdown audit copy, and final HTML. SCAN is a different deliverable. The check is about a reader understanding the paper, not about how polished the page looks. Record `pass`, `fail`, or `unknown` for each gate with a concrete paper or output location. `Unknown` means the evidence needed to judge is unavailable; it is not a softer pass.

## Gates before delivery

| Gate | Pass condition | Failure that blocks a complete deep read |
|---|---|---|
| Source identity and coverage | Title, version, and central sections are known; methods, decisive results or proofs, captions, and relevant appendices were accessible. | Wrong paper, mixed versions without explanation, or only an abstract/partial extraction while claiming a full read. |
| Publication-time research context | The first-publication horizon and source revision are distinguished. A visible chapter before the method explains the field's state, specific predecessor contributions and remaining gaps, and why this paper was proposed. Dates and contribution claims have sources; concurrent and later work are labeled. | Missing background, generic motivation, a bibliography without contributions, unexplained prerequisite terms, retrospective rankings presented as contemporary facts, or later work incorrectly treated as an earlier influence. |
| Problem, value, and narrative continuity | Prose specifies the exact problem, success conditions, and value; connects prior limitations to a plausible hypothesis, actual intuition, method, and evidence in a readable order. Essential concepts precede their use. | A broad topic replaces the research question; importance is only asserted; attractive cards conceal missing causal links; or the prose cannot stand without diagrams. |
| Non-circular idea reconstruction | Before the actual method, a visibly qualified pedagogical reconstruction connects sourced pre-paper knowledge and obstacles to a tentative design hypothesis. It passes the counterfactual check in narrative-logic.md. | This paper's new modules, loss, diagnosis, or results are used as prior premises; invented observations or private author thoughts; unsupported attribution of inspiration; or no explanatory bridge to the idea. |
| Core intuition and worked method | Plain-language intuition precedes technical detail. A real sourced instance, or an explicitly labeled constructed example when needed, is traceable through consequential input → processing → output steps. Training, inference, and evaluation are distinguished. | A slogan or module inventory replaces the idea; examples switch mid-pipeline; essential transformations are missing; or schematic intermediates are presented as a real execution. |
| Key method coverage, salience, and attribution | A source-grounded change inventory accounts for consequential methods, including non-headline changes. Selected mechanisms have descriptive localized subsections and visible pipeline labels; prose explains previous→changed operation, provenance, purpose, mechanics, learning/use, cost, and evidence/limits. | A key change is omitted or only mentioned in a hero/English name list/broad paragraph/folded panel; a diagram hides it; inherited methods are called original; or system-level gains are presented as an untested module's isolated benefit. |
| Mathematical accessibility | Central mathematical foundations and notation are introduced before use; non-obvious steps are justified and connected to the mechanism. Paper proofs and added teaching derivations are distinguished. A missing derivation is explicitly acknowledged without fabricating one. | Unexplained formulas or symbols, an algebraic leap that blocks understanding, a heuristic presented as a proof, or an invented theoretical guarantee. |
| Scientific fidelity | Central mechanisms, equations, figure identities, denominators, numbers, and conditions agree with the source. Worked examples and redraws are labeled. | An invented step, changed objective, wrong figure, unsupported number, or an unresolved source conflict presented as settled. |
| Claim to evidence | Each major conclusion can be followed through a source observation or proof to a bounded interpretation. Whole-system results are not used as isolated component effects. | A headline conclusion has no supporting test/proof, omits a decisive comparator or condition, or states an inference as the authors' result. |
| Four information classes and prose | Local attribution distinguishes paper claims, prior-literature findings, evidence-based inference, and uncertain conjecture. The prose uses concrete, precise claims and natural causal explanations. | Inference or speculation is stated as fact; proposed work is presented as executed; generic filler or stock contrasts replace explanation. |
| Question-driven evidence and takeaways | Central evidence follows question → test/proof → bounded answer; comparator logic and decisive conditions are explained without drowning the reader in numbers. Specific final lessons connect mechanism and evidence. | A leaderboard dump replaces experimental reasoning; a claim has no corresponding question/test; numerical simplification hides a decisive qualifier; or the ending only repeats the abstract or generic praise. |
| Fragile assumption and fair counterexample | A consequential assumption is diagnosed with source-supported reasoning and uncertainty. A concrete counterexample targets a named claim, includes a comparator/test and a result that would defeat the objection, and distinguishes in-scope falsification from stress testing. | Generic weakness lists, unsupported certainty about the weakest assumption, an out-of-scope case called a refutation, or fabricated failures. |
| One-week minimal reproduction | One narrow claim is paired with verified or explicitly conditional access, estimated hardware/time budget, control, metric, decision criterion, seven-day milestones, and fallback. The plan's scope and unexecuted status are clear. | An infeasible whole-system rebuild, no control or decision criterion, hidden resource assumptions, or a proposed proxy presented as a completed replication. |
| Subsequent research search | Actual search is recorded with date, queries, source links and access limits. Primary evidence supports claimed extensions, replications or rebuttals; no verified follow-up is reported with search scope when applicable. | Search is omitted or invented, snippets are treated as verified technical evidence, later work leaks into historical premises, or absence of search results is asserted as proof of absence. |
| Substantive follow-up and novelty discipline | One idea follows from a specific limitation and unmet need, compares with searched close work, and states a new question/mechanism, falsifiable prediction, discriminating test, failure criterion, value, and provisional novelty limits. | A generic component swap, no research question or test, omitted novelty search, or guaranteed originality/success without evidence. |
| Reader completeness | The HTML itself explains the problem, design reason, main mechanism, important formula or proof, and decisive evidence at usable detail. | Essential explanation is replaced by a page number, thumbnail, module list, or decorative diagram that sends the reader back to the PDF. |
| Visual choice and interaction | Core visuals are extracted or faithfully simplified and explained. The default HTML includes a working paper-specific interaction with its action/insight recorded in the audit; controls work offline with pointer and keyboard and leave central explanations readable without scripts. An explicitly requested static format is respected. | Only navigation/provenance toggles or decorative animation; required interaction omitted; a useful branch skipped because Archify was unavailable; invented simulated results; controls fail offline/with keyboard or hide essential facts. |
| Delivery integrity | One offline HTML opens with working figures, math, real chapter links, correct language, and readable narrow-screen access; Markdown audit source retains provenance. | Broken image or math, false navigation, missing caption context, wrong output language, or content available only in a separate artifact. |

If source coverage is insufficient, request the missing paper material or produce a clearly labeled SCAN only when that matches the user's request. A gate that fails in the generated document requires repair and another check. Do not replace source checking with a confident-sounding summary.

## Depth ratings after the gates

Rate each dimension `0` (absent), `1` (named), `2` (explained), or `3` (a reader can reconstruct and use it). Give the passage or figure that earned the rating. The ratings locate revisions; their sum is not a scientific validity score.

| Dimension | What earns a 3 |
|---|---|
| Whole-paper orientation | The reader can state the problem, contribution, and how the major parts connect without mistaking the reading order for the paper's process. |
| Historical placement | The reader can explain what was already possible, which prior contributions mattered, what remained unresolved, and how this paper changed the available choices at its publication time without borrowing later results. |
| Problem and value | The reader can distinguish the narrow research question from its application area, state success conditions, and explain the consequence of the unresolved gap. |
| Idea formation and intuition | From pre-paper premises alone the reader can follow a possible design direction, distinguish it from verified discovery history, then explain the actual idea plainly without relying on module names. |
| Author's design reasoning | For central choices, the reader can say which constraint motivated the choice, how it is expected to help, and what trade-off or assumption remains. Explicit author rationale and editorial inference are distinguishable. |
| Mechanism or proof | Inputs, transformations, variables, and output are clear enough to work one small example or trace one proof dependency. |
| Method changes and salience | The reader can quickly locate and explain the consequential introduced/modified/adopted mechanisms, including meaningful counts, map them to the pipeline, distinguish provenance, and identify tested versus only hypothesized benefits. |
| Mathematical accessibility | The reader can explain the required foundations, follow the central derivation with justified steps, and state its assumptions; or correctly explain that the method provides no theoretical derivation and what essential formulas define. |
| Evidence interpretation | A major claim is linked to the actual task/protocol, comparator, metric or theorem, observation, and justified scope. |
| Grounded takeaways | The reader can state a reusable lesson, the mechanism and evidence supporting it, and a condition under which it may not apply. |
| Critical and experimental reasoning | The reader can identify the fragile assumption, a feasible seven-day test, and a fair counterexample with interpretable positive and negative outcomes. |
| Retrospective and research synthesis | The reader can explain verified subsequent developments and assess a limitation-driven follow-up against close work, testable predictions, and explicit novelty uncertainty. |
| Independent readability | Original figures and diagrams have visible reading cues; the reader can answer key questions using the HTML alone, including on a narrow screen. |

For a theory paper, use proof validity and assumptions where a technical report uses experiments. For a survey, use taxonomy evidence and coverage. Do not manufacture experiments, formulas, figures, or alternatives to fill a template.

## Reader check

First run the prose-only logic check in [narrative-logic](../references/narrative-logic.md) and the audit in [research-extension](../references/research-extension.md). Record each of the 13 answers with concrete passage locations; merely finding headings does not pass. Include the missing-derivation explanation where applicable. Explicitly mark unavailable search, missing access, and unresolved feasibility/novelty rather than calling them verified. This semantic self-check supplements the background checker and renderer tests; it is not automated validation or independent reader evidence.

For question 5, also apply [method-salience](../references/method-salience.md): reconcile the change inventory with the visible method subsections and pipeline. Test a consequential non-headline mechanism when the source contains one; remembering its name alone does not pass. This audit adds no fourteenth explanatory question and cannot be passed by keyword detection.

Include at least one paper-specific question asking which earlier method solved what, why that still left a bottleneck, and why the present paper's direction made sense then. A correct list of names or dates without this causal explanation does not pass the context gate.

Give someone who did not write the atlas only its final HTML and the paper-specific questions in [reader-tasks.md](reader-tasks.md). Record the answer, whether the reader reopened the PDF, what they misunderstood, and where they had to backtrack. The supplied answer keys are based on the published examples and are not a substitute for source verification. An author checking their own page or an automated build passing the gates cannot be described as an independent reader study.

This rubric adapts the idea of explicit hard gates and separately judged depth from [DeepPaperNote's public quality rubric](https://github.com/917Dhj/DeepPaperNote/blob/main/evals/note-quality-rubric.md) to this project's single-file visual reading goal. The design-reasoning dimension responds to [PaperForge's author-reasoning emphasis](https://github.com/FeijiangHan/PaperForge); the claim-to-evidence gate responds to [sodalone/paper-reading-skill's traceability practice](https://github.com/sodalone/paper-reading-skill). The criteria and deliverable here are specific to this project.
